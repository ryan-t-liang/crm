## Automated functional coverage

The browser harness at `qa/prototype-browser-qa.mjs` resets the Demo dataset, captures both normal console failures and handled window errors such as `ResizeObserver` loops, then verifies:

1. HQ dashboard and distributor filtering.
2. Dashboard, Leads, Deals, Contacts, Organizations, Products, Tasks, Distributors, Users, and Settings route entry.
3. Lead creation and navigation to the shared detail workspace.
4. Qualified Lead conversion to a persisted Deal.
5. Deal Product and Add-on updates.
6. Mock email send, internal comment, call log, task creation/completion, note creation, and attachment upload.
7. Activity linkage for comments and completed tasks.
8. LocalStorage state after a full page reload.
9. Mark Won and native Kanban drag-to-stage behavior.
10. Contact notes and attachments.
11. HQ Product editing/Add-on management and distributor read-only behavior.
12. Distributor user navigation and record-scope isolation.
